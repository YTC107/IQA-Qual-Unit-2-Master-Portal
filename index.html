Assessment criteria

Reflective account guide

Make the evidence tell the full story

Write about completed activity

Use the first person and past tense. Explain what you personally planned, sampled, observed, decided or communicated.

Identify the people and work

Name the assessors, learners, qualification, units and assessment evidence involved. Do not leave the assessor to infer this from a filename.

Explain your judgement

State what you checked, what you found, which standards you applied and why you reached your decision.

Record the outcome

Include feedback, agreed actions, responsibilities, review dates and what happened next.

Link exact evidence

Add the precise filename and Dropbox location. If one activity supports several criteria, identify the relevant part rather than duplicating an entire account.

Reuse material carefully

You may reuse relevant writing, but update names, dates, findings and evidence so the account remains accurate for this criterion.
